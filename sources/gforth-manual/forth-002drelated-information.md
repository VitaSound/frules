## Appendix C Other Forth-related information

<span id="index-Forth_002drelated-information"></span> <span id="index-comp_002elang_002eforth"></span> <span id="index-frequently-asked-questions"></span>

There is an active news group (comp.lang.forth) discussing Forth (including Gforth) and Forth-related issues. Its [FAQs](http://www.complang.tuwien.ac.at/forth/faq/faq-general-2.html) (frequently asked questions and their answers) contains a lot of information on Forth. You should read it before posting to comp.lang.forth.

The Forth standard is most usable in its [HTML form](http://forth-standard.org/).
